# SAB Gen Next – Intelichat User Guide

*Last updated: 13 October 2026*

Intelichat answers your questions using the bank's own approved documents (policies, procedures, product guides) and shows you which document and page each answer came from. It is part of the SAB Gen Next platform and works in English and Arabic.

**In short:** log in → pick a Sub Category → ask a clear question → check the referenced sources.

> **Important:** Intelichat only knows what is in the documents uploaded to your Sub Category. It does not search the internet or core banking systems. Always verify critical answers against the referenced source.

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the SAB Gen Next link shared by your team in Microsoft Edge (internal network only).
2. Enter your **SAB User ID** and **password** — the same credentials you use for your SAB systems. Use the eye icon to check your password before submitting.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. On the *Welcome to SAB Gen Next* page, click **IntelliChat** ("Transform documents into knowledge with AI").

## 2. The chat screen at a glance

| Where | Control | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left panel | **Search conversations** | Find a past chat by typing a word from it |
| Left panel | **New Chat** | Starts a fresh conversation with no previous context |
| Left panel | Chat history | Your past chats, grouped by date — click one to reopen it |
| Top right | **Select Sub Category** | Chooses which set of documents Intelichat answers from (required) |
| Top left | Home icon | Returns to the SAB Gen Next welcome page |

| Bottom bar | **EN / AR** toggle | Switches the chat language between English and Arabic |
| Bottom bar | **Ask anything** box | Type your question and press Enter or the send arrow |

## 3. Categories and Sub Categories (your access)

Access works on two levels, and you only see documents you are permitted to see:

- **Category** — your business area, e.g. *WPB*. This is assigned to you based on your role.
- **Sub Category** — a topic within your Category, e.g. *Credit Card*, *Finance*, *Electronic Services*, *Accounts & Segments*, *Branches*, *Complaints*.

**Before every question, select the right Sub Category** from the dropdown at the top right. Intelichat searches only the documents in that Sub Category. If none is selected, you will not get an answer; if the wrong one is selected, you may get "no information found" or a less relevant answer.

*Example:* asking how to change a credit card payment method? Select **Credit Card** first, not *Finance*.

## 4. Asking a question and reading the answer

1. Select the **Sub Category**.
2. Choose the language with the **EN / AR** toggle.
3. Type your question in **Ask anything** and press Enter.

When the answer comes from your documents, a **Referenced Sources** list appears under it. Each line shows the **document name** (e.g. *CCD-TCC01-220724.25*) and the **page number** on the right. Use these to open the original document and confirm the details — especially for limits, fees, timelines and eligibility.

If no Referenced Sources are shown, the answer is not backed by your documents. Treat it with caution, rephrase the question, or check that the correct Sub Category is selected.

## 5. Conversations: follow-ups, new chats and search

- **Follow-up questions:** Intelichat remembers the current conversation, so you can continue naturally — e.g. *"What if the customer has both Visa and Mastercard?"* after asking about payment methods.
- **Start a New Chat when the topic changes.** Earlier questions can confuse the answer to an unrelated new one. One topic = one chat.
- **Find old chats:** type a keyword in **Search conversations**, or scroll the date-grouped history and click a chat to reopen it.

## 6. Uploading and viewing documents

**Supported files:** PDF only, in English or Arabic. Text-based PDFs work best; blurry scans or images of text may not be read correctly.

**To upload:**

1. Select the **Sub Category** the document belongs to — you can upload only to Sub Categories within your own Category.
2. Click the upload icon (↑) in the bottom bar.
3. Click **Choose Files**, pick one or more PDFs, then click **Submit** (or **Clear** to start over).

**To view documents:** click the document icon (**Show all documents**). The list shows File Name, Category, Sub Category, Uploaded By, Uploaded Time and User Name, with a filter on each column.

Good practice: use clear file names with the document code and date, and don't upload outdated versions — Intelichat may answer from whichever version it finds.

## 7. Tips for getting the right answer

Think of Intelichat as a colleague who has read only your Sub Category's documents. It first finds the passages that best match your words, then writes an answer from them. The closer your question is to the wording in the documents, the better the answer.

| Do | Instead of | Ask like this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Be specific | "Credit card?" | "What is the procedure to change a credit card payment method?" |
| Use the bank's terms | "Unblock my account" | "How can a dormant account be reactivated?" |
| Ask one thing at a time | "Card fees, limits and how to close it?" | Three separate questions, one after another |
| Give the situation | "Can I do it?" | "Can a staff charge card be converted to a credit card?" |
| Name the product or channel | "How to transfer?" | "What is the daily transfer limit on Al-Awwal Online?" |
| Ask for a format when useful | — | "List the steps…", "Summarise in 3 points…" |

More tips:

- **Rephrase, don't repeat.** If the answer is off, use different words or the term used in the document.
- **Ask in the same language as the documents** where possible; switch EN / AR if answers seem weak.
- **Don't ask about things not in the documents** — live balances, customer data, today's rates or news.
- **Never enter customer personal data** (ID numbers, card numbers, account numbers) in your question.
- **Always check the Referenced Sources** before acting on an answer.

## 8. Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| No response to my question | Select a Sub Category, then ask again |
| "No information found" or a vague answer | Check the Sub Category; use more specific words or the document's terms |
| Answer mixes up an earlier topic | Click **New Chat** and ask again |
| No Referenced Sources shown | The answer isn't from your documents — rephrase or verify manually |
| Upload fails | Make sure the file is a PDF and a Sub Category in your Category is selected |
| I can't see a Category or Sub Category I need | Request access from your manager or via **Contact Us** |
